      How to test

      1. Lower your volume, open Headphone Test and confirm the volume warning.
      2. Play left, right and both channels. Each side should sound only on its own earcup.
      3. Try the stereo, frequency, bass, noise and balance tests.
      4. Open Microphone Test, press Start, allow access, then speak and watch the level meter.
      5. Record in Recording Studio, play it back and download it.
      6. Run the noise and speech checks in Audio Diagnostics.

      Privacy

      Your microphone audio stays on your device. GearBlizz does not upload, store or transmit your recordings.

      The microphone only turns on when you press a button, and it is released when you stop or leave the microphone sections.

      Stereo balance

      Play a sound, then move the slider until it feels centered. A centered slider means equal level in both ears; a large offset can point to a driver mismatch, a poor seal or an uneven ear.
